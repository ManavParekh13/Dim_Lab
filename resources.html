Frequently Asked Questions

How does the AI determine when to irrigate?
The AI analyzes real-time soil moisture, soil temperature, and your specific crop growth stage. It uses these parameters to generate a highly specific recommendation on whether to irrigate, delay, or skip watering.
Do I need an internet connection?
For the advanced Gemini AI insights, yes. However, if you lose connectivity, the system automatically falls back to an offline rule-based advisory engine, ensuring you always get guidance.
How are the moisture percentages calculated?
Sensors placed at the root zone (approx 30-45cm deep) measure volumetric water content. Readings below 35% typically indicate high water stress for sugarcane.
What languages are supported?
Currently, we support English, Marathi, Kannada, and Hindi. The AI generates contextual advice native to each language.
