Frequently Asked Questions

How does the AI irrigation model calculate water requirements? ▼

Our proprietary AI analyzes real-time telemetry from IoT sensors, comparing current soil moisture and temperature against optimal baselines for the specific crop stage to determine precise watering needs.

What data sources does the system use? ▼

We combine real-time soil moisture and temperature data from field sensors, hyper-local weather forecasts, and the current developmental stage of your sugarcane crop.

How should growers apply the advisory recommendations? ▼

Growers should review the daily AI advisory on their dashboard. If an action like "Irrigate soon" is recommended, farmers can optimize pump operation times to match the suggested duration, saving water and electricity.
